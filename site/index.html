SRC-X.5

An open record of claims that anyone can check. Each record states what was predicted and by which criteria, which files were fixed and when, when it ran, and what came out. Every record passes seven checks; what fails is quarantined, never published, and nothing is deleted. This page re-checks every record in your browser: it recomputes each record's identity with SHA-256 and verifies the chain, without trusting the server.
